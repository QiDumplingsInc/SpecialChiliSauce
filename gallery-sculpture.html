Sculpture

雕塑

Nothing three-dimensional yet. The paintings already care a great deal about weight and surface, the skin of a pomegranate and the wet flank of a fish, so sculpture is a natural direction rather than a departure. This page is here waiting for the first piece.

目前还没有立体作品。画作本身已极为在意重量与表面质感——石榴的果皮、鱼身湿润的侧腹——因此雕塑是一个自然的方向，而非另起一路。本页在此等待第一件作品。
